Hey everyone! Welcome to **Binarygap Log**.

Why the name "Binarygap"? For me, it's all about bridging the gap between where you are today and where you want to be tomorrow—one deliberate, steady step at a time. It’s a personal reminder to keep showing up, staying curious, and growing every single day.

I've always been someone who gets genuinely pumped about building things from scratch. But lately, I’ve fallen in love with everything that happens *before* the finished product: diving down research rabbit holes, letting my imagination run wild, and translating messy thoughts into crisp blueprints and actionable plans.

That’s exactly what this space is for. Consider it a live, open notebook of my explorations, deep-dive research notes, and the conceptual frameworks I'm actively tinkering with.

Glad to have you along for the ride. Let's build something great!
